Sidecar Apps is building a family of consumer AI apps

Each app starts with a specific task people already spend time on. Across the portfolio, we combine on-device AI, also called edge AI, with cloud AI to turn everyday information into something people can act on.

AI on the device

Phones can handle tasks like reading text from a photo. Doing that work on the device can make it quicker and reduce how much data needs to be sent.

AI in the cloud

The cloud helps when a task needs a more capable model or outside information. Sending fewer routine requests to the cloud helps control the cost of serving each user.

CasaSense is our first app

The water heater at my rental broke, and nobody had tracked how old it was. I wrote the first version of CasaSense on a flight to Bangalore to help people keep those details handy.

A label photo starts an appliance record that a household can use for maintenance, warranty questions, or repairs. A clear label can be read on the phone and sent as text. In one controlled engineering test, 16 simultaneous submissions of the same photo shared a single cloud recognition call.
