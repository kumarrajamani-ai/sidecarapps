About Sidecar Apps

We’re building a family of consumer apps that help people handle everyday tasks with less manual work.

We start with moments when people spend more time finding, typing, or sorting through information than acting on it. We use AI to handle that work so people can get to the decision they came to make.

How we build

On your device

We handle routine tasks like reading text from a photo on your device when possible. That helps keep common tasks quick and reduces how much data has to leave your device.

In the cloud

We use the cloud when a task needs more analysis or information the device doesn’t have.

You decide what to share

We design our apps so people can review important details and decide what to save or share.
