About Sidecar Apps

We build consumer apps that use AI to make everyday tasks easier.

Our apps handle tasks like reading a photo on your phone when they can. The cloud helps when a task needs more analysis or outside information. That keeps common tasks quick and reduces how much data needs to leave your phone.

CasaSense is our first app

CasaSense helps homeowners and landlords keep an appliance inventory, stay on top of maintenance, and share the details when something needs fixing.

Our founder wrote the first version on a flight to Bangalore after a water heater broke at their rental and nobody knew how old it was.
