About Sidecar Apps

Sidecar Apps is building a family of consumer apps that use on-device and cloud AI to make everyday tasks easier.

We use AI on your phone when it can handle the task there. The cloud helps when a task needs more analysis or information from outside the device. This keeps common tasks quick and reduces how much data needs to leave your phone.
