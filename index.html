About Sidecar Apps

We build consumer apps that use AI on the device and in the cloud to make everyday tasks easier.

AI runs on your phone when it can do the work there. The cloud helps when a task needs a more capable model or information from outside the device. Using both helps us keep common tasks fast, send less data, and manage the cost of serving each user.

CasaSense is our first app. It turns a photo of an appliance label into a record you can use for maintenance, warranty claims, and repairs. Our goal is to bring that same approach to other everyday tasks where people spend too much time gathering information before they can act.
