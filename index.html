01 / CAPTURE

Point. Snap. Organize.

Photograph an appliance or label to capture its brand, model, and serial number.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Know its story.

Keep age and warranty information together. Start with an estimate if you're unsure.

03 / STAY INFORMED

Keep an eye on recalls.

Items are checked against US Consumer Product Safety Commission recalls.

04 / PLAN

See what's coming.

View a replacement timeline and estimated cost ranges, so you can plan ahead.

05 / SHARE

Get everyone on the same page.

Invite a partner, manager, or renter. Ask your contractor for a quote by email link, with no account required.
